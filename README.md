# Tag Arena

**What it is** - A tiny online tag game with multiple gamemodes and lots of features!

**TRY IT HERE** - [Game](https://tag-arena-2gu4.onrender.com)

Either invite your friends to play or play alone with bots or use 2 different tabs. To play with friends, simply create a lobby and give the other players the invite code or link, and with that, they can join you.

**Controls** - WASD or Arrows to move or drag on the screen. Space or shift to dash.

**Scoring** - This is simple - you gain points while you are free and you lose them when you are IT, and the winner is the one with the most points when the timer runs out. There is also a freeze tag mode where IT freezes everyone instead.

**Behind The Scenes** - I built the server authoritative at 30 ticks a second with plain websockets, so speed hacks don't work. It's all just a few files, `npm install` then `node server.js` runs it locally.

**Notes** - The website is hosted on Render free tier (I planned to use Hackclub Nest but still waiting approval)
